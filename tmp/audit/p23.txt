Shill Bidding Protection Shill bidding is one of the biggest risks in an auction platform. The original design proposes rule-based mechanisms that prevent sellers from bidding on their own listings using secondary accounts or linked IP information. The platform should potentially monitor: ● Seller-bidder relationship ● Account relationships ● IP patterns ● Device signals ● Unusual bidding patterns ● Repeated bidding against specific users ● Bid timing ● Bid frequency Important: IP address alone should not be treated as definitive proof , because multiple legitimate users can share networks. It should be one signal among several. 26. Listing Moderation Listings should be checked before publication. The original proposal includes: ● Text filtering ● Image filtering ● Counterfeit detection
