1. Executive Summary BidKori is a real-time online auction marketplace designed initially for the Bangladeshi market. The platform enables individuals and businesses to list products and sell them through live, synchronized auctions, allowing buyers to compete through real-time bidding. The platform addresses a major gap in Bangladesh's resale ecosystem: although product auctions already occur through Facebook groups, comments, live streams, and other informal channels, these environments lack dedicated auction infrastructure, automated bidding mechanisms, reliable auction timers, transparent bid histories, fraud protection, seller tools, and structured post-auction workflows. BidKori converts this informal auction behavior into a structured digital marketplace. The platform combines: ●​ Real-time bidding ●​ Automated auction management ●​ AI-assisted product listing ●​ AI-powered platform guidance ●​ Seller and buyer management ●​ Business seller support ●​ Fraud and shill-bidding prevention ●​ Listing moderation ●​ Auction analytics ●​ Flexible seller monetization The original product concept focused primarily on C2C resale. The updated product expands this to include B2C, allowing medium-sized businesses—particularly second-hand and refurbished-product sellers—to operate as professional sellers on BidKori. The updated monetization strategy also introduces a GMV-based Premium subscription, allowing frequent sellers to pay a fixed monthly subscription in exchange for a defined monthly transaction-value allowance instead of paying the standard percentage fee on every qualifying sale.

4. Problem Statement 4.1 Fixed-Price Inefficiency Traditional e-commerce platforms generally rely on fixed prices or negotiation. This works well when sellers know the appropriate market price. However, many products have highly variable market values. Examples include: ●​ Used smartphones ●​ Gaming consoles ●​ Laptops ●​ Cameras ●​ PC components ●​ Sneakers ●​ Collectibles ●​ Vintage products ●​ Rare items Auction-based price discovery can allow market demand to determine the final selling price. 4.2 Unstructured Social Bidding A significant amount of informal bidding can happen through social platforms. Typical workflow: Seller posts product ↓ Users comment bids ↓ Someone places a higher bid ↓ Another user responds ↓ Seller manually determines winner

BidKori is a Bangladesh-focused real-time auction marketplace that enables individuals and businesses to sell products to consumers through transparent, synchronized live bidding. The platform combines a WebSocket-powered auction engine with AI-assisted listing creation, conversational platform guidance, seller/business tools, marketplace governance, fraud prevention, and flexible monetization. Casual sellers can use a pay-as-you-sell model, while frequent and business sellers can subscribe to GMV-based Premium plans that provide a defined monthly sales-value allowance and additional seller benefits. And the strategic positioning is: Facebook gives Bangladesh the audience. Bikroy gives Bangladesh classified listings. BidKori gives Bangladesh the infrastructure for trusted live auctions. That, in my view, is the core identity of the product now.
