8. Seller Workflow Step 1 — Seller Registration Seller creates an account. The platform determines whether the seller is: ● Individual ● Business Business sellers can eventually provide additional verification information. Step 2 — Product Creation Seller provides: ● Product title ● Category ● Images ● Basic information ● Condition ● Keywords Step 3 — AI Description Generation The AI service generates a structured description. For example: Product: iPhone 13 128GB Input: Used Blue 128GB Battery health 87% Minor scratches
